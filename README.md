Gift Card

A simple and heartfelt HTML gift card website designed to comfort a girl who is feeling sad. It features a beautiful card design with a sweet, thoughtful message to remind her that she is appreciated, loved, and never alone.

Features

- Beautiful and creative gift card design.
- Heartwarming and comforting message.
- Simple HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Clean and responsive interface.

Purpose

A small digital gift made with love to bring a smile to someone's face and brighten their day.
